# Changelog

## Before 0.3.0

Until 0.3.0 the clients lived in three repositories, each with its own
version line. Their changelogs moved here unchanged:

- Node, `@cruglobal/flags` 0.1.0 to 0.1.3: [docs/node/CHANGELOG.md](docs/node/CHANGELOG.md)
- Python, `cru-flags` 0.1.0 to 0.1.3: [docs/python/CHANGELOG.md](docs/python/CHANGELOG.md)
- Ruby, `cru-flags` gem 0.1.0 to 0.2.0: [docs/ruby/CHANGELOG.md](docs/ruby/CHANGELOG.md)

From 0.3.0 on, all four packages, Go included, release together under one
version.
